UX challenges, in priority order

Ordered by how fast a miss hurts Lena.

1
Find the one who needs her in under three seconds
Exceptions first. The fleet is a summary, never the main event. Color only where attention goes.
5
One driver, one story
One card with all its reasons. Over the limit reads differently from approaching. Acknowledging never hides act now.
2
A countdown she can act on
Live and derived from duty segments. Minute precision. Never flaps at the 30:00 boundary.
6
Drill in without losing the shift
The route file is a page, not a modal. The co-pilot stays open. One click back to the board.
3
Say “we do not know”
Stale looks stale. Data age and legal exposure stay two numbers. A correction is shown, not swapped in.
7
A co-pilot that cannot disagree with the board
One ranked list feeds both. Plain language. The no-match reply is still useful.
4
A fix that does not create the next one
Preview both drivers. Confirm. Undo. The picker refuses anyone who would enter act now.
8
Every empty state explains itself
Nothing to flag. A filter that matches nothing. No candidate with capacity. Each one says why and what to do.
